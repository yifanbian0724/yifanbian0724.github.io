Face Mirror

Make a face. See the movements, and what studies say about them.

A synthetic clay head copies the movements of your face, live. Face Mirror names each one as an action unit of the Facial Action Coding System (FACS), the standard way to describe facial movements. For the combination you hold, it shows what published studies in Face Value's evidence base report: when people felt a state, when they posed one, and what observers saw. It never says what you feel.

Runs in your browser. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

    How it works

    1. Tracking, on your device. A face-tracking model scores 52 small facial movements about 30 times a second, inside this browser tab. It learns your usual resting face as you go, or you can set it with "Set my resting face".MediaPipe Face Landmarker · WebAssembly, WebGL · ARKit-style blendshapes
    2. Mirroring. The scores move the matching expression shapes of a synthetic 3D head, turned like a reflection. No one's face is shown.ICT-FaceKit · three.js
    3. Naming movements. A fixed table turns the scores into estimated FACS action units. A movement counts once it stays above its threshold for a moment.35 action units · first estimates, not FACS coding
    4. Reading the evidence. When a combination holds for most of a second, the panel shows Face Value's evidence for it, graded by the independent study samples behind it. Blinks, gaze and head turns are shown but not read.Face Value evidence base, October 2026

    Limits

    • Estimates, not FACS coding by trained human coders. The tracker was built to animate avatars. Some movements have no score at all (for example AU23, lip tightener), and others register weakly or as their neighbours (cheek raising as lid tightening, nose wrinkling as upper-lip raising).
    • Lighting, glasses, beards, head turns and the camera angle all change the scores.
    • The evidence is about combinations of actions in groups of people under study conditions. It cannot say what one person feels at one moment.

    Privacy

    The camera starts only after you agree. Its image is processed in this browser tab and is never recorded, stored or sent, and neither is anything measured from it. The page sets no cookies and has no analytics; its security policy lets it load code only from this site and jsDelivr. Stopping the camera, or closing the tab, ends everything.

    Use your camera?

    • Your camera image is analysed only inside this browser tab.
    • Nothing is recorded, stored or sent: no images, no measurements, no analytics.
    • Face Mirror shows the facial movements it sees and what studies report about them. It never says what you feel.
    • The face tracker (about 14 MB, by Google) downloads once and runs on your device.
    • Stop at any time with "Stop camera", or by closing the tab.
