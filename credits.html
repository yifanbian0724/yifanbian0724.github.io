The faces on the home page

The wall behind my name shows 49 photographs: 22 candid photos from Open Images (Flickr) and 27 photos of public figures from Wikimedia Commons. Each is cropped, and its tag shows the facial action units that LibreFace, an open detector, found in it. The labels are the model’s, not a judgement of anyone’s feelings, and some are wrong.
